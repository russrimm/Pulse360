## Design Context

### Users
IT admins and Microsoft 365, Azure, Fabric and Power Platform consultants who track service changes that affect their tenants. They scan many update streams (Message Center, Azure updates, release plans, MSRC, lifecycle) to decide what needs action, communicate it to stakeholders, and plan around dates. They work at a desk, often with many tabs open, and need to triage fast and trust what they read.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, authoritative. Tagline: "Stay ahead. Stay informed. Stay in control." Voice is plain and factual, like a well-run operations desk. No hype, no urgency theater.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark-first, with a fully supported light theme.
- Restrained, utilitarian clarity: strong typographic hierarchy, tinted neutrals, one disciplined accent color, semantic status colors (critical, warn, ok, info) used only for meaning.
- Anti-reference: the generic SaaS/AI look (purple gradients, glowing cards, identical card grids).
- Existing token system in `src/app/globals.css` (canvas, surface, line, ink, accent, critical, warn, ok, info) is the source of truth.

### Design Principles
1. Triage first: the most actionable and time-sensitive items are the most visible; scanning beats browsing.
2. Calm authority: color and motion carry meaning only; status colors are never decoration.
3. Show provenance: source, date and freshness are always visible so users can trust the data.
4. Dense but legible: favor tight, well-ordered lists and clear hierarchy over cards for everything.
5. Honest states: loading, empty, stale and error states are explicit, specific and recoverable.
6. Use US English in all UI copy.
